Functions:
--------
Functions are subroutines which can be used as ana when needed in our programs. It is a separate piece of code, which is used for performing a particular task. 
It is easier to code and find errors in a function.
This approach is also known as Modular approach or module-based approach.

def countDigits(n):
	count =0
	
	while(n>0):
		count+=1
		n-=1
	return count
