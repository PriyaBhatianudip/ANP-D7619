->remove empty strings from the given list:-
input items = ["Laptop", "", "TV", "Mouse", "", "", "keyboard", "", "Tab"]

output
items = ["Laptop", "TV", "Mouse", "keyboard","Tab"]

enumeration :  means listing or counting items one by one , usually using a number or index. 
enumerate():  is used to get both index and value while iterating  over a sequence.

Syntax:
items = ["Laptop", "TV", "Mouse", "keyboard","Tab"]
for index, name in enumerate(items):
	print(f"{index} : {name}")
0 : Laptop
1 : TV
